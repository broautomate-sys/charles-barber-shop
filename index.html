Barber in Mannheim

Our barber shop in Mannheim

Charles Barber Shop is in the heart of Mannheim, at T3 23 in the city centre. Since 2013, Charles has looked after his customers with men’s haircuts, fades and beard grooming, and also offers braiding for women and haircuts for kids.

Professional men’s haircuts

At Charles Barber Shop in Mannheim you get precise men’s haircuts with clean lines, from classic cuts to modern looks. As a men’s barber in Mannheim, we take the time for your hair type, your head shape and your personal style.

Modern fade cuts

Skin fade or a seamless blend from short to long: our barbers in Mannheim create fade cuts with clean, even transitions and a sharp finish.

Professional beard grooming

We trim and shape your beard with defined lines that suit your face, and we offer a close, careful shave. Beard trimming in Mannheim, done precisely and with care.
